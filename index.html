About the project

The idea behind the name

A welfare home is rarely associated with happiness. Happy Hill is an intention: that these spaces should be places of growth, stimulation, connection and care, and not merely institutional management.

The project is implemented by DCIP Kozhikode together with the Social Justice Department (SJD) and the Department of Women and Child Development (WCD), which administer the six welfare homes covered under the programme.

Its work is to help close the distance between what these homes are, and what they should be, for the people who live in them.

“

The core problem in welfare homes is not resource scarcity alone. It is social exclusion.

Happy Hill answers it with a structured partnership model: partner colleges, expert institutions, and DCIP’s administrative access.
